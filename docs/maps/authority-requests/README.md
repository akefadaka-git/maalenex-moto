## Purpose

This folder contains recipient-specific request letters and the submission evidence tracker for obtaining authoritative administrative registers, boundary data, change history and commercial/offline reuse permissions.

## Files

- `01-regional-administration.md` — regional custodian identification, official hierarchy, boundary records, transition decision and rights.
- `02-south-omo-zone.md` — current South Omo roster, Maale parentage, kebeles and boundary data.
- `03-ari-zone.md` — current Ari roster, affected historical units and boundary data.
- `04-maale-woreda.md` — Maale official identity, parentage, kebele roster and local gazetteer verification.
- `SUBMISSION-TRACKER.md` — status, receipt and data-integrity log.

The combined source package remains at `docs/maps/ADMINISTRATIVE-DATA-REQUEST-PACKAGE.md`.

## Recommended submission order

1. Regional office first: confirm the competent administrative/GIS data custodian and the proper official submission channels.
2. South Omo and Ari zone administrations: obtain signed current rosters, effective dates and boundary datasets.
3. Maale Woreda: verify local administrative identity, parentage, kebeles and settlement names.
4. Reconcile all returned information against the 2020 historical dataset without treating that dataset as current.

## Submission controls

- Complete applicant identity and legal capacity; the company is in formation.
- Owner approval and signature required before submission.
- Send through an official, verified channel; preserve stamped receipt, registry number or official acknowledgement.
- Do not send confidential personal information through an unverified address.
- Preserve original authority files and correspondence in access-controlled storage; do not place restricted or personal data in this public repository.
- Record SHA-256 for each received source file before transformation.
- Do not activate live map, routing, offline distribution or commercial display until provenance, technical quality, rights and field validation are accepted and separately approved.
